# Operations Walkthrough — Interview Script

A spoken version of [OPERATIONS_DISCOVERY.md](OPERATIONS_DISCOVERY.md) and the Mar-Kov
questions, ordered the way the paint actually moves. Meant to be held in one hand and read
aloud, not filled in.

**Record it.** Let him talk. The best answers come from the tangents.

Two things to say at the start:

> "I'm not testing anything and nothing here changes how you work. I'm trying to write
> down how it actually happens — including the parts that are messy or that people work
> around — so the new system fits the real process instead of a tidy version of it."

> "If the honest answer is 'it depends' or 'nobody really knows', say that. Those are the
> most useful answers I can get."

---

## Opening — let him set the scene

1. Walk me through what happens from the moment we decide we need paint, right through to
   it going out to a customer. Take as long as you want.

*Then let him talk. Everything below is for filling gaps he didn't cover.*

---

## 1. Ordering the paint

2. Who decides we need more paint, and what makes them decide that?
3. Who raises the PO to TCC, and what goes on it?
4. Does TCC come back and confirm, or do they just start making it?
5. How long between the PO and the paint being ready?

## 2. TCC makes it

6. What gets typed into Mar-Kov, and who does it — during the batch or after?
7. Does a batch get a number, and does that number follow the paint afterwards?
8. How do you know how much came out of a batch — gallons, totes, weight?
9. **How close is that to what we ordered?** Is it usually spot on, or does it swing?
10. What happens if a batch is bad?
11. How do we find out it's ready?

## 3. Bulk — driving it over

12. Talk me through the semi run. Who drives, is there paperwork?
13. Does anyone count or sign for it when it lands here?
14. Who updates the PO to what actually arrived — and when?
15. Does TCC's invoice come with the paint or later?

## 4. Aerosol — the canning company

*This is the part I understand least. Let him take his time.*

16. Talk me through the aerosol route from start to finish.
17. Who is the canning company, and where are they?
18. **While the paint is sitting at their place, is it ours or TCC's?**
19. Do we send them a PO? Do they bill us?
20. What do we send them — just paint, or cans and caps and labels too?
21. What comes back, and how do we count it — cases, pallets?
22. **A tote of paint doesn't turn into an exact number of cans. How much goes missing?**
23. How long do they usually have it?
24. Do they ever hold stock for us between runs?
25. Does the batch number end up on the cans?

## 5. It arrives here

26. Who takes it in, and what do they check?
27. Do we count it, or take the paperwork's word for it?
28. Where does it go — do we need to know which rack or bay?
29. What happens when the count is wrong? Who hears about it?

## 6. When someone else hauls it

30. When do we use a shipping company instead of our semi?
31. Who books it, and who pays — us or TCC?
32. Is that freight part of what the paint costs us, or just an expense?

## 7. Out the door

33. Who picks an order, and what are they working from?
34. Does anyone check it before it ships?
35. Who books the outbound carrier, and when do we get a tracking number?
36. When do we need a bill of lading, and who makes it?
37. **When do you consider that stock gone — when it's picked, loaded, or invoiced?**

---

## Mar-Kov

38. Which bits of Mar-Kov do we actually use, and which do we ignore?
39. **Do you trust the inventory numbers in it?** (We know QuickBooks' numbers aren't
    reliable — is Mar-Kov different?)
40. Are recipes and formulations kept in there, or somewhere else?
41. Does it know anything about the canning step, or does that happen outside it?
42. Does it work out what a batch cost, and does anyone look at that?
43. What does it do badly? What do people work around?
44. Is it hooked up to QuickBooks today — and to which company file, TCC's or USSC's?
45. Who looks after it, and who would we ring about connecting it to something?

---

## The split

46. The idea is that Mar-Kov keeps everything up to the finished batch at TCC, and the new
    system takes over once it's finished goods here. **Does that match how you think about
    it?**
47. Is there anything on the wrong side of that line?

---

## Counting — new, and only he can answer it

*Added 24 September 2026, once the counting screens were built.*

53. We can now count stock properly — blind, so the counter isn't shown what the system
    expects. **When could we do a full count of each building, and who would do it?**
54. After that, how often should we be counting things — and should it be the expensive
    stock, the fast-moving stock, or just everything in turn?
55. **Who should own a count?** Somebody has to look at "we thought 40, we counted 37" and
    decide what it means.
56. When a delivery from TCC doesn't match the PO, who should be told?
57. Who should be allowed to correct a stock figure? It's the one action that changes the
    count with nothing physical happening.
58. What bays and racks are in 730, and how are they labeled? Right now the whole building
    is one place as far as the system is concerned.
59. When a transfer between buildings arrives short, who chases it?
60. Is there a rule about returned paint going back on the shelf — anything opened is
    scrap, say — or is it a judgment each time?

---

## Close — usually the best part

48. What goes wrong most often in all of this?
49. What do people work around, or keep in their head?
50. Is there a spreadsheet or a whiteboard doing a job a system should be doing?
51. **If one thing in this whole chain got tracked properly, what would help you most?**
52. Anything I haven't asked that I should have?
